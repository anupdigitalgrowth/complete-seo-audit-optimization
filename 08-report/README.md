# Final SEO Audit Report PDF (`seo-audit-report.pdf`)

> **Note:** The actual compiled PDF report will be generated and saved in this directory as `seo-audit-report.pdf` upon completion of the real audit data entry.

---

## Required PDF Document Structure & Outline

When compiling the final client-facing PDF presentation report, ensure it follows this 16-section agency-standard blueprint:

1. **Cover Page** (Project Title, Website URL, Auditor Name/Title, Date)
2. **Executive Summary** (High-level narrative, core achievements, overall health summary)
3. **Website Overview** (Business context, CMS stack, target audience, site architecture)
4. **Audit Methodology & Tools** (Screaming Frog, GSC, GA4, Ahrefs, PageSpeed Insights)
5. **Technical SEO Audit** (Crawlability, indexability, XML sitemaps, robots.txt, canonicals, HTTPS, mobile, speed)
6. **On-Page SEO Audit** (Title tags, meta descriptions, headings, URL structures, internal links, images)
7. **Keyword Research & Mapping** (Keyword discovery matrix, search intent categorization, URL mapping)
8. **Competitor Analysis** (Competitor benchmarking matrix, content gaps, keyword opportunities)
9. **Google Search Console Insights** (Clicks, impressions, CTR, position trends, index coverage)
10. **Key Findings Summary** (Prioritized matrix of critical, medium, and low-priority issues)
11. **Strategic Recommendations** (Actionable fix blueprints and developer instructions)
12. **Implementation Execution Log** (Summary of optimizations applied during campaign)
13. **Before vs. After Optimization Proof** (Side-by-side evidence screenshots and performance fixes)
14. **Measurable Results & Outcomes** (Quantifiable growth metrics in organic traffic and visibility)
15. **30/60/90-Day Action Plan** (Strategic roadmap for ongoing SEO growth and content expansion)
16. **Conclusion & Sign-off** (Final remarks, contact links, portfolio links)
