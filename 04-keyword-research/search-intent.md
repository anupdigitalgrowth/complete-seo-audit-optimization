# Search Intent Analysis

## Purpose

This document provides a comprehensive search intent classification analysis to categorize target keywords by user motivation, ensuring content strategies align with what searchers expect to find in Google Search Results.

## Intent Categories

### Informational

- **Definition:** The user is seeking knowledge, answers to specific questions, guides, tutorials, or educational explanations.
- **Common Modifiers:** *How to, what is, guide, tips, tutorial, examples, history of, why does*
- **Target Content Formats:** Blog posts, how-to guides, infographics, video tutorials, wiki pages.

### Navigational

- **Definition:** The user is attempting to find a specific website, brand page, portal, or web address.
- **Common Modifiers:** *Brand name, login, homepage, contact page, support portal, pricing page*
- **Target Content Formats:** Brand homepage, specific login pages, official directory pages.
- **Keyword Examples:** `[ADD EXAMPLES LATER]`

---

### Commercial Investigation

- **Definition:** The user intends to buy or convert in the future and is comparing options, reading reviews, or evaluating solutions.
- **Common Modifiers:** *Best, top 10, vs, comparison, review, cheap, features, alternatives*
- **Target Content Formats:** Comparison tables, product reviews, listicles, buying guides, case studies.
- **Keyword Examples:** `[ADD EXAMPLES LATER]`

---

### Transactional

- **Definition:** The user is ready to complete an action, make a purchase, subscribe, sign up, or download immediately.
- **Common Modifiers:** *Buy, order, discount, coupon, price, quote, hire, checkout, download*
- **Target Content Formats:** Product sales pages, checkout pages, service booking pages, free trial sign-up forms.
